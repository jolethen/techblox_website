How to Join Techblox

Follow these 4 simple steps to connect to the world.

01

Launch Luanti / Minetest

Download and install the free open-source client from luanti.org.

02

Open "Join Game" Tab

Navigate to the multiplayer menu tab inside your client main screen.

03

Enter Address & Port

Address: play.techblox.com
Port: 30000

04

Register & Spawn

Choose your username and password, then click Join to spawn directly into the game!
